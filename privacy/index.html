Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BS Group Holdings Pty Ltd, trading as The AI Agency Builder ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website theaiagencybuilder.com and related services.

Information we collect

When you submit a form on our website, we may collect your name, email address, and phone number. We also collect basic technical information such as your browser type and the pages you visit.

How we use your information

  • To contact you by phone, email, or SMS about the free course and getting your AI agent set up.
  • To provide, personalise, and improve our services and content.
  • To send you updates, offers, and service messages where you have consented.

SMS / text messaging

By providing your phone number and ticking the consent box on our forms, you agree to receive calls and text messages (SMS) from The AI Agency Builder related to your enquiry and access, including service updates and, where you opt in, promotional messages.

  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help.
  • Consent to receive messages is not a condition of any purchase.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or SMS consent with any third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes. Mobile opt-in information is never shared with third parties for marketing.

Sharing your information

We use trusted service providers (such as our CRM, hosting, and messaging providers) to operate our business. These providers only process your information to provide services to us and are bound to keep it confidential. We do not sell your personal information. As stated above, mobile opt-in data and SMS consent are never shared with third parties for marketing.

Data retention

We keep your information only for as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Contact below).

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may withdraw consent to marketing at any time. Contact us using the details below.
