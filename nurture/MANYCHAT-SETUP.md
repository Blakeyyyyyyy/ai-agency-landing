# ManyChat → VSL funnel (5 min, on your phone)

Your Jarvis reels are the top of funnel. This turns a comment into a click on the VSL.

## The keyword
Automation → Keywords → New. On the IG account your Jarvis reels post from.
- Trigger word: **JARVIS** (also add: jarvis, AGENCY, agency)
- Applies to: comments on any Post or Reel + DMs

## The DM it sends (paste this)
> Opening DM:
> "Yes — here it is. This is the exact AI agent that runs my agency, and the free build that shows you how to make your own. Watch this 👇"
> Button: **Watch it** → URL: https://theaiagencybuilder.com/?src=ig-[reelname]
>
> Follow-up if unclicked (1 hr later):
> "Still keen? The whole thing's free, takes about 9 minutes to watch 👇" → same link

## The reel CTA
End every Jarvis reel with: **"Comment JARVIS and I'll send you the free build."**
Say it out loud AND put it on screen. One keyword, every reel feeds the same page.

## Why src=ig
The ?src= tag flows into the waitlist row, so you can see which reel drove which signup. Change `[reelname]` per reel if you want per-reel numbers, or leave it as `ig` for all.

## Do NOT
Promise SMS in the DM to Indian followers — SMS won't deliver there. The VSL page handles that; the DM just needs to get them watching.
