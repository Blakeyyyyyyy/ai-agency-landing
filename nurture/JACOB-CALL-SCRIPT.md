# Jacob — the setter call

Work `jacob-call-list.csv` top-down: the 13 "Yes ready" first, then newest. Non-India only.
Goal of the call: confirm fit, get them signed up on Base44 through Blake's link, book them into the course.
Not a hard sell. They asked for this. You're helping them claim it.

## Before you dial
Have open: Blake's Base44 partner link, and an incognito browser window.
First-click attribution: they MUST sign up through Blake's link, incognito, fresh email, no VPN — or the credit is lost. You walk them through that live.

## Open
"Hey [name], it's Jacob from Blake's team — you put your hand up for the free AI agency build, the Jarvis one. Got two minutes? Good. This is just to get you set up and make sure it's a fit."

## Qualify (soft — the form already did most of it)
1. "What made you sign up — what are you hoping this does for you?"
2. "Have you tried anything like this before?" (dropshipping/agency/etc — normal, builds rapport)
3. "You good with the $50-a-month platform cost? That's the only cost, it's not ours, it's the tool Jarvis runs on." — if a hard no, they're not a fit, be kind and move on.

## The setup (the actual point of the call)
"Alright, I'll get you in now. I'll send you a link — open it in a private/incognito window with an email you've got handy. That's how the free access ties to your account."
Walk them through: click link → sign up on Base44 → confirm. Stay on the line until it's done.

## Close
"You're in. The course opens the second you're set — you build your own Jarvis first, then the services you sell. Blake's team is in the community if you get stuck. Go build it tonight while it's fresh."

## If no answer
One SMS: "Tried to ring you about your free Jarvis setup — was me, not spam. Try again tomorrow, or text a time that suits. Jacob (Blake's team)"
Two misses → drop them to the email nurture (sequence B).

## Log every call in GHL
Move the card: Called → Booked → Signed up → Lost. Note the outcome. That's how Blake sees what's working without asking you.
